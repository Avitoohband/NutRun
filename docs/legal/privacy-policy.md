# NutRun Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-24  
**App:** NutRun (`com.avitoohband.nutrun`)

## Summary

NutRun helps you track training, nutrition, walks, supplements, and progress. This policy describes what we collect, why we use it, and your choices.

## Information we collect

- **Account:** email address and authentication identifiers from Firebase Authentication.
- **Profile and health inputs:** birth date, sex, height, weight, activity level, goals, and calorie targets you enter.
- **Training data:** workouts, exercises, sets, schedules, and rest-timer preferences.
- **Nutrition data:** meals, foods, macros, water, and supplement schedules.
- **Walk data:** walk sessions, steps, distance, timestamps, and route points when location is used during recording.
- **Device data:** app version, coarse diagnostics for crashes, and operational telemetry needed to run sync and billing.
- **Purchases:** subscription status verified through Google Play and the NutRun backend (not full payment card data).
- **Ads (free plan):** Google AdMob may collect advertising IDs and consent-related signals when ads are shown.

## How we use information

- Provide core app features (logging, charts, reminders, sync).
- Verify subscriptions and apply entitlements.
- Store route geometry privately for your account when cloud sync is enabled.
- Show ads only on the free ad-supported plan, after consent where required.
- Improve reliability and security.

## Location

Location is used to record walk routes during active walk recording. Route data is stored for your account and is not used for unrelated tracking.

## Health Connect

NutRun requests Health Connect permissions only from the Progress screen. Imported/exported data types are disclosed in the Play Health apps declaration.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Service providers include Google (Firebase, Maps, Play Billing, AdMob), and NutRun cloud hosting for sync/MCP when enabled.

## Retention and deletion

Data remains until you delete it or delete your account. Account deletion removes cloud records per backend policy and clears local account data on the device.

## Your choices

- Edit profile and logged data in the app.
- Sign out without losing per-account tutorial preferences.
- Delete account from Profile (requires typing your email).
- Manage ad consent through the Google UMP flow when shown.
- Upgrade to remove ads.

## Children

NutRun is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

For privacy requests, contact the NutRun project maintainer at the support email published in Play Console.

## Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected in Play Console and this document.
